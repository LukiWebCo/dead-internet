How much of the new web is written by AI?

An independent, longitudinal dataset of AI-generated content in English-language news on the public web. Everything here is a population-level estimate with a confidence range, never a verdict on a single document. Data as of 2026-10-10 (UTC) · methodology v1.2. New here? Start with the simple explanation.

AI-style vocabulary in English news peaked at 2.16× its pre-ChatGPT level in December 2023 and is now 0.76× its pre-ChatGPT level (September 2026, 95% range 0.64–0.90). A falling index does not by itself mean less AI writing: these words come from 2023–24 studies, and newer models use some of them less. The common-word list and the drift band help tell the two apart.

Beyond news

News is polished and took up AI early, so other kinds of writing may move differently. Each one has its own pre-ChatGPT baseline and ranges and is never mixed with the news numbers. Same word lists, same method. Forums first, then scholarly papers, then the wider web.

Hacker News

comments and posts on Hacker News, a tech discussion site. Compared with Hacker News before ChatGPT (1.00). Circles: one week per month since 2022-12 (2,000 texts each); squares: weeks collected as they happen.

Stack Overflow

answers on Stack Overflow, the programming Q&A site. It banned AI-written answers in December 2022 and its volume fell after ChatGPT, so the population itself changed. Collecting its pre-ChatGPT baseline now: 8 of 20 weeks (5,668 answers). The chart appears here when the baseline is ready.

arXiv papers

abstracts of new research papers on arXiv (physics, maths, computer science and more), dated by first submission. Papers revised after ChatGPT are left out of the baseline, because arXiv serves the latest version's abstract. Compared with arXiv papers before ChatGPT (1.00). Circles: one week per month since 2022-12 (2,000 abstracts each); squares: weeks collected as they happen.

PubMed abstracts

abstracts of new medical and life-science papers in PubMed, by the week their record was created. Journal papers are written months before they appear, so this series lags. Courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Compared with PubMed abstracts before ChatGPT (1.00). Circles: one week per month since 2022-12 (2,000 abstracts each); squares: weeks collected as they happen.

Methodology and version

Methodology v1.2. Documents come from Common Crawl CC-NEWS, sampled at random and kept with the exact location of the raw record. Estimates are population-level only; we do not label individual documents. Read the method.
