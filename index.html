What this page will show

No numbers are shown until there is a real baseline. We are collecting English news from before ChatGPT (2022-11) to compare against.

  • Word-shift index: how much more often a documented list of words (10 style words from published studies) appears than in the pre-2022-11 baseline, with a 95% range. 1.00 means no change.
  • Drift band: how far the same index moved between 2019–20 and 2021–22, before ChatGPT. Shifts inside it can be topic or outlet change rather than AI.
  • Leftover-phrase floor: the share of documents that contain text such as “As an AI language model”. It is a minimum: most AI text has no such trace.
  • Week by week for both, once the baseline holds 20 weeks and 10000 documents.

Methodology and version

Methodology v1.2. Documents come from Common Crawl CC-NEWS, sampled at random and kept with the exact location of the raw record. Estimates are population-level only; we do not label individual documents. Read the method.
